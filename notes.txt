This is to practice the alevin-fry pipeline done in my work for Dr.Bacher lab. Instead of just being the alevin-fry run, however, we will include some downstream analysis to make it an end-to-end pipeline rather than just preprocessing.

What is the difference between R1 and R2?
  - R1 -> the who and which; details a cell barcode (CB) for which cell the molecule came from
                                     a unique molecular identifier (UMI) tagging the original mRNA molecule
  - R2 -> the what; the actual sequence to be mapped to the transcriptome; the gene

Salmon alevin output? a CB-by-gene count matrix where each entry is a UMI count
Process?
  1) Read only the R1 and find the unique barcode sequences.
    - Creates a frequency distribution, but why? The goal is to reassign error variants to the 'correct' variant
    - 'Correct' variants occur at a much greater frequency than error variants, so we reassign the lower frequency variants to the higher frequency 'correct' variant.
    - This process effectively recovers data from the error variants and groups them with the 'correct' variants.
  2) Read R2, match UMI barcodes that most likely come from the same variant
    - Remove duplicates by collapsing all shared reads into one count while accounting for UMI collisions
  3) Post-abundance-estimation CB whitelisting
    - Removes potentially junk CBs post-hoc to refine barcodes.
